About me

Hi, I'm Bill! I'm an undergraduate student at Columbia University studying Computer Science. I am broadly interested in deep learning, mechanistic interpretability, and trading/market design.

Some of my past experiences have included an internship in quant finance at Jane Street, mechanistic interpretability research with SPAR, and plasma modeling with Columbia Fusion Research Center. I have also worked/interned at a few crypto startups, including Liquid and a HIP-3 exchange.
